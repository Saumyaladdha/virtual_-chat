## Geography (Bhugol) Common Mistakes — Class 11 & 12 Board Exam Students ke liye

---

MISTAKE 1 — DIAGRAMS NA BANANA
Population Pyramid, Water Cycle ya Agriculture ke types mein diagrams/flowcharts na banana ek badi galti hai. Geography mein bina diagram ke answer "incomplete" lagta hai — examiner ka impression weak banta hai.

Galat tarika:
'Expanding Population' ke baare mein sirf paragraph likh dena — koi diagram nahi.

Sahi tarika:
Ek triangular diagram banao jahan base wide ho (high birth rate dikhane ke liye). Use ek small box mein enclose karo. Diagram ke parts clearly label karo — Age groups on Y-axis, Population % on X-axis.

Aur bhi examples:
- Water Cycle → arrows ke saath flow diagram (Evaporation → Condensation → Precipitation → Runoff)
- Types of Agriculture → simple flowchart ya classification tree

Rule: Jo bhi chapter mein "types", "cycle", "stages", "pyramid" wale concepts hain — wahan diagram banana compulsory samjho. 2-minute ka diagram answer ki "professional value" badha deta hai aur extra marks milne ke chances bhi badhte hain.

Applies to: Population chapter (pyramid), Water Resources (water cycle), Agriculture (farming types), Industries (location factors).

---

MISTAKE 2 — DATA MEIN GALTI (WRONG STATISTICS)
Important data (jaise Literacy Rate ya Sex Ratio) galat likhna marks kaatata hai. Examiner exact figures ke baare mein strict hota hai — ek galat number se credibility jaati hai.

Galat tarika:
"Bharat mein Literacy Rate 80% hai." (yeh fact galat hai)

Sahi tarika:
"2011 ki Census ke anusaar, Bharat ki Literacy Rate 74.04% hai."

Smart tip — agar exact figure yaad nahi:
"2011 ki Census ke anusaar, Bharat ki Literacy Rate approximately 74% hai." — yeh likhna safe hai.

Aur common data mistakes:
- Sex Ratio: 940 females per 1000 males (2011 Census) — 900 ya 960 mat likho
- Population density: approx 382 persons/sq km (2011) — exact yaad nahi toh "approximately 380" likho
- Galat figure completely mat likho — guess karna sabse bura option hai

Rule: Numbers mein agar doubt ho — "approximately", "around", "as per 2011 Census" use karo. Completely wrong figure = marks loss. Honest approximation = safe.

Applies to: Population, Human Development, Resources chapters — jahan Census data aur statistics frequently aate hain.

---

MISTAKE 3 — MAP PAR OVERLAPPING
Ek hi jagah par bahut saari cheezein dikhane ki koshish mein map ko messy kar dena. Symbols aur locations overlap ho jaati hain — examiner kuch bhi clearly nahi padh paata.

Galat tarika:
Iron, Coal aur Rivers sab ek hi pencil se mark kar dena — koi differentiation nahi.

Sahi tarika:
- Iron region → Triangle (▲)
- Coal region → Square (■)
- River → Blue wavy line (ya single wavy pencil line)
- Hamesha map ke corner mein Index/Legend banana compulsory hai

Legend example:
▲ — Iron Ore Region
■ — Coal Region
〰 — Major River

Rule: Map banane se pehle decide karo — kaun se 2-3 symbols use karoge. Phir consistently wohi use karo. Bina index ke examiner confuse ho jaata hai — marks directly iss ek step se bachte hain.

Applies to: All map-based questions — Mineral distribution, Agriculture zones, River systems, Industrial regions.

---

MISTAKE 4 — CASE STUDIES KI GENERAL EXPLANATION DENA
Specific case studies (jaise Indira Gandhi Canal area) mein sirf general baatein likhna kam marks dilata hai. Specific locations aur unse hue actual changes ka mention karna zaroori hai.

Galat tarika (general — kam marks):
"Nahar aane se kheti achhi hone lagi aur hariyali aa gayi."

Sahi tarika (specific — zyada marks):
"Indira Gandhi Nahar ke aane se Rajasthan ke Thar Desert region mein ecological changes hue. Cotton aur Groundnut jaisi crops ka production badha, lekin excessive irrigation se Waterlogging aur Soil Salinity ki problem bhi paida hui hai."

Specific impact + specific problem = full marks.

Aur bhi examples:
- Narmada Bachao Andolan ka answer sirf "dam se log hat gaye" se nahi banta — displacement numbers, states affected, rehabilitation policy mention karo
- Green Revolution case study mein Punjab-Haryana region specifically naam lo, not "some states"

Rule: Case study questions mein 3 cheezein zaroori hain — (1) specific location/region name, (2) specific positive impact, (3) specific problem/limitation. General answer = general marks.

Applies to: Agriculture, Water Resources, Disaster Management, Regional Development chapters.

---

MISTAKE 5 — PRACTICAL WORK MEIN CALCULATION KI GALTI
Statistics aur Mean/Median wale questions mein chhoti si calculation mistake se poore 5 marks kat jaate hain — yeh sabse avoidable galti hai.

Galat tarika:
∑f ya ∑fx ki calculation mein jaldi karte hain aur addition mein galti ho jaati hai — final answer galat nikalta hai.

Sahi tarika:
Step 1: Saari values carefully add karo — ek baar slowly
Step 2: Reverse check karo — ulta jod ke verify karo answer sahi hai ya nahi
Step 3: Final answer ek clean box mein likho: Mean (x̄) = 45.5

Rough work ke liye:
Answer sheet ke right side mein ek margin banao — examiner ko clearly pata chale ki rough work kahan hai aur final answer kahan. Rough work dikhana important hai — step marks milte hain.

Rule: Practical questions mein calculation haste nahi — woh 5 marks direct calculation accuracy par hain. Reverse check ki aadat daalo — 30 seconds lagti hai lekin marks bachti hai.

Applies to: Practical Geography — Mean, Median, Mode, Population density calculations, Bar graphs, Pie charts.
