## History (Itihas) Common Mistakes — Class 11 & 12 Board Exam Students ke liye

---

MISTAKE 1 — STORY LIKHNA VS. BULLET POINTS LIKHNA
Students aksar apna answer ek lamba paragraph ya story ki tarah likhte hain. Board exam mein answers ko bullet points aur sub-headings mein na likhna sabse badi galti hai — examiner ke paas time nahi hota poori story padhne ka.

Galat tarika:
"Asahyog Andolan 1920 mein shuru hua tha, ismein logon ne sarkari naukariyon ko chhod diya aur videshi kapdon ko jalaya gaya kyonki Gandhi ji chahte the ki..." (ek continuous story)

Sahi tarika:
Andolan ka Swaroop (Nature): Yeh ek non-violent resistance tha.
Bahishkar (Boycott): Sarkari schools, colleges aur videshi vastuon ka tyaag.
Swadeshi: Khadi ke upyog par emphasis dena.

Rule: Jab bhi 4+ marks ka answer likhna ho — pehle 2-3 sub-headings socho, phir har heading ke neeche 2-3 bullet points. Examiner ek second mein samajh jaata hai tum jaante ho — marks milte hain.

Applies to: All chapters with descriptive answers — Nationalism, Colonialism, Partition, World Wars, etc.

---

MISTAKE 2 — FACTS KI KAMI (LACK OF SPECIFIC EVIDENCE)
Answer mein specific names, places aur evidence ka zikr na karna ek badi galti hai. Sirf general statements likhne se poore marks nahi milte — examiner evidence dhundta hai.

Galat tarika:
"Harappa ke log trade karna jante the."

Sahi tarika (facts ke saath):
"Harappa ke log Mesopotamia ke saath trade karte the, jiske evidence hume Lothal ke port aur Harappan seals se milte hain."

Aur bhi examples:
- Sirf "Akbar ek tolerant ruler tha" mat likho — "Akbar ne Din-i-Ilahi aur Sulh-i-Kul ki policy apnayi, aur Fatehpur Sikri mein Ibadat Khana banaya jahan different religions ke scholars aate the" likho
- Sirf "Vijayanagar ek powerful empire tha" mat likho — specific temples, trade routes, foreign travellers (Ibn Battuta, Paes) ka reference do

Rule: Har claim ke saath ek specific example ya evidence zaroor do. General statement + specific evidence = full marks.

Applies to: Ancient History, Medieval History, Modern History — har chapter mein.

---

MISTAKE 3 — TIMELINE KI GALTI (WRONG CHRONOLOGY)
Events ke years ya sequence galat likh dena ek common aur costly mistake hai — examiner immediately marks kaat leta hai.

Galat tarika:
Asahyog Andolan ka year 1925 likh diya (sahi: 1920-22)

Sahi tarika:
- Agar exact year yaad nahi hai toh decade ya century use karo — "early 1920s" likhna better hai
- Galat year bilkul mat likho — examiner turant pakad leta hai
- Better hai accurate approximation se galat precision

Aur common timeline mistakes:
- Simon Commission: 1927 (1928 nahi — yeh India visit 1928 mein tha)
- Dandi March: March 1930 (1929 nahi)
- Partition of Bengal: 1905 (1906 nahi)

Rule: Jo years pakke yaad hain wohi likho. Agar doubt ho toh "in the early/mid/late [decade]" likhna safe hai — examiner us par marks nahi kaatega. Guess mat karo.

Applies to: Modern History — all movements, acts, and events with specific dates.

---

MISTAKE 4 — MAP MARKING MEIN CONFUSION
Map par locations ko wrong state mein mark karna, ya pencil ki jagah pen use karna — jisse correction possible nahi hoti.

Galat tarika:
'Chauri Chaura' ko Bihar mein mark kar diya — yeh completely wrong hai.

Sahi tarika:
Chauri Chaura — Uttar Pradesh ka Gorakhpur district (yahan farmers aur police ke beech 1922 mein incident hua tha)

Map marking ke rules:
- Hamesha sharp pencil use karo taaki galti hone par erase kar sako
- Location mark karne ke baad uska naam clearly likhna mat bhoolo
- Dot/cross ke saath naam likhna compulsory hai — sirf dot se marks nahi milte

Common map mistakes:
- Lothal → Gujarat mein hai (Rajasthan mein nahi)
- Taxila → Pakistan (present-day) mein hai
- Ajanta Caves → Maharashtra mein hai (Madhya Pradesh mein nahi)

Rule: Map questions mein pencil = lifeline. Hamesha pencil use karo, location ke saath naam likho, aur compass direction (N/S/E/W) se cross-check karo.

Applies to: Ancient History (Harappan sites), Medieval History (empires), Modern History (movement locations).

---

MISTAKE 5 — SOURCE-BASED QUESTIONS KO SKIP KARNA
Textbook ke beech mein jo 'boxes' diye hote hain — jisme primary sources aur quotes hote hain — unhe na padhna ek badi galti hai. Board exam mein inse directly questions aate hain, lekin students inhe difficult samajh kar ignore kar dete hain.

Galat approach:
Box mein Bernier ka quote diya hua hai — student ne poora box skip kar diya kyunki woh difficult English mein tha.

Sahi approach:
Agar question "Bernier's views on India" par hai, toh box mein diya hua uska specific quote (jaise 'State Land Ownership theory') apne answer mein include karo — examiner par strong impression padta hai aur extra marks milte hain.

Source-based question mein marks kaise milte hain:
- Source ko padhkar uska context batao (kab likha, kisne likha)
- Source se direct evidence ya quote diya answer mein
- Source ki limitation bhi batao agar question poocha — yeh higher order thinking dikhata hai

Rule: Har chapter ke boxes aur insets ko underline karo pehli reading mein. Yeh 5 marks ke source questions ka seedha source hain — board mein ye questions skip karna matlab guaranteed marks chhod dena.

Applies to: All chapters — especially Colonialism, Nationalism, Partition chapters jahan primary sources frequently aate hain.
