Accounts / Commerce Common Mistakes — Class 11 & 12 Board Exam Students ke liye

---

MISTAKE 1 — T-SHAPE FORMAT SAHI SE NAHI BANANA
Accounts ka sabse basic rule hai ki T-account sahi format mein banana — heading centred, Dr. left side, Cr. right side, columns properly aligned. Board exam mein format galat ho toh presentation marks seedha kaat lete hain.

Galat tarika:
Dr.    Cr.
To drawings 2000   By balance b/d 10000
To interest 500    By profit 3000  To cash 1000
Total 13500        Total 14000

(Koi alignment nahi, entries mix ho gayi, totals bhi galat)

Sahi tarika:

                     Current Account
Dr.                                                    Cr.
Particulars         ₹       Particulars               ₹
To Drawings      2,000      By Balance b/d         10,000
To Interest        500      By Profit & Loss        3,000
To Balance c/d  10,500
Total            13,000      Total                  13,000

Yaad rakho:
- Account ka naam heading mein centred likhna zaroori hai
- Dr. hamesha left side, Cr. hamesha right side
- Dr. side pe entries "To" se shuru hoti hain
- Cr. side pe entries "By" se shuru hoti hain
- Dono taraf ke totals hamesha equal hone chahiye

Rule: Account banana shuru karne se pehle mentally decide karo — heading, Dr/Cr labels, column names, phir entries. Seedha entries mat likhna shuru karo.

Applies to: All chapters — Current Account, Capital Account, Profit & Loss Account, Trading Account, Balance Sheet.

MISTAKE 2 — "TO" AUR "BY" PREFIX BHOOL JAANA
Dr. side pe "To" aur Cr. side pe "By" likhna compulsory hai. Bina iske examiner marks kaat leta hai kyunki yeh accounting ka basic convention hai.

Galat tarika:
Dr. side mein likha: "Drawings 2,000" (bina "To" ke)
Cr. side mein likha: "Balance b/d 10,000" (bina "By" ke)

Sahi tarika:
Dr. side mein: "To Drawings 2,000"
Cr. side mein: "By Balance b/d 10,000"

Rule: Ek simple trick — Dr. = "To" (T se T yaad rakho), Cr. = "By" (C se "By" — Credit waala By). Yeh dono prefix kabhi mat bhoolna.

Applies to: Ledger accounts, Current Account, Capital Account, Profit & Loss Account — har jagah jahan T-format use hota hai.

MISTAKE 3 — FORMULA LIKHNA SKIP KARNA — SEEDHA CALCULATION KARNA
Students aksar formula skip karke seedha calculation likh dete hain. Agar answer galat aaya toh zero milta hai. Formula likhne se step marks milte hain — bhale answer galat ho.

Galat tarika:
= 12,000 × 10% × 6/12
= ₹600
Interest on Drawings = ₹600

(Koi formula nahi likha — sirf calculation hai)

Sahi tarika:
Interest on Drawings = Total Drawings × Rate × Time/12
= 12,000 × 10% × 6/12
= ₹600

Aur bhi examples:
- Goodwill = Super Profit × Purchase Years — pehle formula, phir values substitute karo
- Net Profit = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses — formula pehle likhna zaroori hai

Rule: Har numerical mein pehla kaam hai — formula likhna. Phir values dalna. Phir calculation. Yeh 3-step order kabhi mat todna — step marking yahi se milti hai.

Applies to: Interest on Capital, Interest on Drawings, Goodwill calculation, Depreciation, Ratio Analysis — sabhi numerical chapters.

MISTAKE 4 — ACCOUNTS KA BALANCING GALAT KARNA
Account balance out karna bhool jaana ya totals galat likhna — yeh ek mistake se poora account spoil ho jaata hai. Board mein ek bhi figure galat ho toh dono totals mismatch ho jaate hain.

Galat tarika:
Dr. side total: ₹13,500
Cr. side total: ₹14,000

(Totals equal nahi hain — account balance nahi hua)

Sahi tarika:
- Pehle dono sides ki entries add karo alag alag
- Jo side kam ho, usmein "Balance c/d" (Dr. mein) ya "Balance b/d" (Cr. mein) likhke barabar karo
- Phir dono totals equal ho jaate hain

Rule: Jab dono totals mismatch lage — wapas jaao aur har entry check karo. Galti aksar ek entry ki hoti hai. Total mein haste nahi — carefully add karo.

Applies to: Ledger accounts, Current Account, Capital Account, Trading Account, P&L Account.

MISTAKE 5 — KAAM DIKHANA (WORKING NOTES) NAHI LIKHNA
Complex calculations mein students sirf final answer likhte hain bina working dikhaye. Board exam mein working notes ke alag marks hote hain — jo working nahi dikhata uske marks jaate hain.

Galat tarika:
Goodwill = ₹45,000
(Koi calculation nahi, koi formula nahi)

Sahi tarika:
Working Note:
Average Profit = (30,000 + 40,000 + 50,000) / 3 = ₹40,000
Normal Profit = Capital × Normal Rate = 3,00,000 × 10% = ₹30,000
Super Profit = Average Profit − Normal Profit = 40,000 − 30,000 = ₹10,000
Goodwill = Super Profit × Purchase Years = 10,000 × 4.5 = ₹45,000

Rule: Jab bhi koi bhi figure aaye jo directly question mein nahi di — us figure ka working note likhna zaroori hai. "WN-1, WN-2" karke side mein mark karo aur answer mein reference do.

Applies to: Goodwill valuation, Partnership questions, Company accounts — sabhi chapters jahan intermediate calculations hoti hain.
