Political Science (Rajniti Vigyan) Common Mistakes — Class 11 & 12 Board Exam Students ke liye

---

MISTAKE 1 — TECHNICAL TERMS KA ISTEMAL NA KARNA (LACK OF TERMINOLOGY)
Political Science ek "theory-heavy" subject hai. Ordinary words ki jagah subject-specific technical terms use karna answer ko academic depth deta hai. Simple conversational language se answer ka impact bahut kam ho jaata hai.

Galat tarika (ordinary language):
"America bahut powerful hai aur woh poori duniya par apna influence daalta hai."

Sahi tarika (technical language):
"Contemporary world politics mein America ki Hegemony clearly visible hai, jo sirf military mein hi nahi balki Structural aur Cultural fields mein bhi reflect hoti hai."

Aur bhi examples:
- "Logo ki alag alag soch hai" ki jagah: "Ideological pluralism democratic systems ki pehchaan hai"
- "Desh aapas mein mil ke kaam karte hain" ki jagah: "Multilateral cooperation through international institutions like UN, WTO"

Rule: Har answer mein kam se kam 2-3 technical/subject-specific terms use karo. Textbook ke bold words yaad rakho — wahi terms examiner dhundta hai.

Applies to: All chapters — Cold War, Federalism, Democracy, International Relations, Globalization.

---

MISTAKE 2 — CARTOON AUR IMAGE-BASED QUESTIONS MEIN SIRF DESCRIPTION DENA
Cartoon ko sirf dekhna aur uske peechhe ke satire ya political message ko na samajhna ek badi galti hai. Students aksar image ka description toh de dete hain, lekin uska political meaning explain nahi kar paate.

Galat tarika (sirf description):
"Ismein ek dragon bana hai aur ek cycle hai jiske wheel par dollar ka sign hai."

Sahi tarika (political meaning ke saath):
"Yeh cartoon China ke economic rise aur global trade mein uske badhte dominance ko represent karta hai, jahan 'dragon' China ka symbol hai aur 'cycle with dollar sign' Capitalism aur Communism ke beech ke internal conflict ko dikhata hai."

Rule: Cartoon question mein do cheezein zaroori hain — (1) kya dikh raha hai (brief), (2) iska political meaning/satire kya hai (main answer). Sirf description = half marks.

Applies to: Chapters on Cold War, Contemporary World Politics, Democracy — jahan NCERT textbook mein cartoon boxes hote hain.

---

MISTAKE 3 — CONTEMPORARY EXAMPLES NA DENA (OUTDATED ANSWERS)
International Relations ke questions mein current events ya treaties ka example na dena answer ko outdated bana deta hai. Examiner ko lagta hai student sirf ratta maar raha hai.

Galat tarika:
Global Warming par sirf definition likhna — "Global warming ek problem hai jisme temperature badh raha hai."

Sahi tarika:
"Global Warming ek critical challenge hai. COP28 Summit (Dubai, 2023) mein countries ne fossil fuels se transition ka decision liya. Paris Agreement ke targets ke under 1.5°C warming limit ko maintain karna goal hai."

Aur bhi examples:
- India-Pakistan relations mein Shimla Agreement (1972) ke saath recent Line of Control tensions mention karo
- UN reforms chapter mein UNSC permanent membership debate (India's case) ka example do

Rule: Har answer mein ek current example ya recent event zaroor add karo. Yeh simple 1 line ka addition examiner par "aware student" ka impression create karta hai.

Applies to: International Relations, Environment, Globalization, Security chapters.

---

MISTAKE 4 — MAP MEIN CODING KI GALTI
Political Science ke map questions mein letters (A, B, C, D) ke saamne correct state ka naam likhne mein galti karna common aur costly mistake hai — ek galti se poora question wrong ho jaata hai.

Galat tarika:
Letter C ke saamne "Maharashtra" likh diya jabki correct answer "Andhra Pradesh" tha (linguistic movement waala state).

Sahi tarika:
Map legend carefully padho — pehle identify karo ki letter kis region par hai, phir naam likho.

Common Political Science map mistakes:
- Linguistic reorganization states galat map karna
- North-East India ke states confuse karna (Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram)
- J&K aur Ladakh ko post-2019 correctly mark na karna

Rule: Map question mein pehle easy locations mark karo. Agar kisi letter ke baare mein confident nahi ho toh usse last mein karo. Spelling bhi carefully likho — "Andhra Pardesh" se marks katenge.

Applies to: Chapters on Federalism, Nation Building, Regional Aspirations — jahan state-level map questions aate hain.

---

MISTAKE 5 — 6-MARK QUESTIONS MEIN CONCLUSION NA LIKHNA
Bade answers mein Introduction aur end mein Conclusion na likhna answer ko incomplete bana deta hai. Examiner ko lagta hai ki answer adhoora hai — chahe content kitna bhi achha ho.

Galat tarika:
Answer mein saari points likhi, UN ke baare mein 5 paragraphs likhe — aur phir abruptly khatam kar diya.

Sahi tarika (conclusion ke saath):
"In conclusion, yeh kaha ja sakta hai ki halanki United Nations apni shortcomings aur need for reforms ke liye criticism face karta hai, lekin war aur pandemic jaise difficult times mein yeh duniya ke liye ek 'indispensable platform' bana hua hai."

Conclusion kaise likhein:
- 2-3 lines — zyada nahi
- "In conclusion / To summarize / Thus" se shuru karo
- Main point ka summary do — naya point mat add karo
- Agar ho sake toh balanced view do (positive + limitation)

Rule: 6-mark answer = Introduction (2 lines) + Body (points/bullets) + Conclusion (2-3 lines). Teen parts ke bina answer structural marks khoega. Conclusion likhna 1 minute ka kaam hai — kabhi skip mat karo.

Applies to: All 6-mark questions — Democracy, Federalism, International Organizations, Cold War chapters.
