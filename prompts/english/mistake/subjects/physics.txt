## Physics Common Mistakes — Class 11 & 12 Board Exam Students ke liye

---

MISTAKE 1 — UNIT BHOOL JAANA
Yeh sabse common aur silly mistake hai. Student sahi calculation karte hai, sahi answer likhte hai — par unit nahi likhta. Board exam mein iske liye pura answer galat ho jaata hai, marks nahi milte.

Galat tarika:
E = 5

Sahi tarika:
E = 5 N/C

Aur bhi examples:
- v = 20 (galat) → v = 20 m/s (sahi)
- F = 10 (galat) → F = 10 N (sahi)
- I = 2 (galat) → I = 2 A (sahi)

Rule yaad rakho: Number ke saath unit nahi hai toh answer zero. Board exam wale ek bhi mark nahi dete bina unit ke.

Applies to: Electric Field, Force, Velocity, Current, Resistance, Pressure, Energy — basically har numerical chapter.

---

MISTAKE 2 — ALAG UNITS SAME FORMULA MEIN DAAL DENA
Problem mein ek value cm mein hoti hai, doosri m mein — aur student bina convert kiye seedha formula mein daal deta hai. Result bilkul galat aata hai.

Galat tarika:
u = 1 m, f = 15 cm — dono alag units mein formula mein daal diya

Sahi tarika:
Pehle convert karo: u = 100 cm, f = 15 cm — phir formula lagao
Ya dono ko metre mein: u = 1 m, f = 0.15 m

Yeh mistake aksar:
- Mirror formula (1/v + 1/u = 1/f) mein hoti hai
- Lens formula mein hoti hai
- Kinematics ke questions mein hoti hai jahan height metres mein aur speed cm/s mein di ho

Rule: Formula lagaane se PEHLE ek baar check karo — kya saari values ek hi unit mein hain? Nahi hain toh pehle convert, phir substitute.

Applies to: Ray Optics, Kinematics, Electrostatics.

---

MISTAKE 3 — SIGN CONVENTION BHOOL JAANA
Cartesian sign convention Physics ka backbone hai. Concave mirror ki focal length negative hoti hai — par students positive likh dete hain. Board exam mein yeh ek mark ya do mark ki galti seedha cut kar deti hai.

Galat tarika:
f = 15 cm (concave mirror ke liye positive likh diya)

Sahi tarika:
f = −15 cm (concave mirror mein focal length hamesha negative)

Yaad rakho:
- Concave mirror → f negative
- Convex mirror → f positive
- Concave lens → f negative
- Convex lens → f positive
- Object distance u → hamesha negative (object hamesha left side hota hai)

Rule: Pehle diagram banao, sign convention decide karo — PHIR values substitute karo. Bina sign ke formula seedha galat ho jaata hai.

Applies to: Ray Optics (Mirrors, Lenses), Motion in One Dimension.

---

MISTAKE 4 — RAY DIAGRAM MEIN LABELLING NAHI KARNA
Student ray diagram banata hai — but principal axis ka naam nahi likhta, F aur C mark nahi karta, rays pe arrows nahi lagataa. Board exam mein diagram ke alag marks hote hain, aur bina labelling ke aadhe marks jaate hain.

Galat tarika:
Ek diagram banaya, koi bhi label nahi — na F, na C, na arrows, na image point

Sahi tarika:
- Principal axis clearly mark karo
- F (focus) aur C (centre of curvature) dono mark karo
- Har ray pe arrow lagao (light ki direction dikhao)
- Image ki position clearly mark karo aur batao real hai ya virtual

Rule: Diagram ke saath labels nahi hain toh diagram adhoora maana jaata hai Board exam mein. Drawing ke baad 30 second labelling mein lagao — marks pakke ho jaate hain.

Applies to: Ray Optics, Wave Optics.

---

MISTAKE 5 — KINEMATICS MEIN POSITIVE DIRECTION FIX NAHI KARNA
Motion problems mein students seedha formula lagaate hain bina yeh decide kiye ki kaun si direction positive hai. Isse g, u, s ke signs galat ho jaate hain aur answer completely wrong aata hai.

Galat tarika:
Ball upar phenki gayi 10 m height se, initial speed 10 m/s —
Student ne: g = +10 m/s², u = +10 m/s², s = 0 liya (height consider hi nahi ki)

Sahi tarika:
Pehle decide karo — upar ki direction positive hai.
Toh: u = +10 m/s, g = −10 m/s², s = −10 m (ground 10 m neeche hai starting point se)

Rule: Har kinematics problem mein SABSE PEHLE ek line likho — "Taking upward as positive" ya "Taking downward as positive" — phir ussi hisaab se saari values ka sign lagao. Yeh ek line likhne se half mistakes automatically avoid ho jaati hain.

Applies to: Motion in One Dimension, Projectile Motion, Gravitation.

---

MISTAKE 6 — FORMULA RATT LIYA, SAMJHA NAHI — TWIST AAYA TOH PHANS GAYE
Board exam mein kabhi kabhi simple twist deke poochha jaata hai — formula wahi hota hai par condition thodi alag hoti hai. Jo students sirf ratta maarte hain woh yahan phans jaate hain.

Galat approach:
Electric dipole ka formula E = kp/r³ ratt liya. Question aya: "Kya yeh formula 2 nm doori ke dipole ke liye 10 nm aur 50 nm pe valid hai?" — student ko pata hi nahi kya answer karna hai.

Sahi approach:
Samjho ki E = kp/r³ sirf tab valid hai jab r >> 2a (point dipole condition). Agar r aur 2a comparable hain toh exact formula lagta hai.
Axial point pe: E = 2kp/r³, Equatorial point pe: E = kp/r³ — dono alag hain.

Rule: Derivation padhte waqt ek kaam zaroor karo — poochho "Yeh formula kab valid hai? Kab nahi?" Condition samajh lo toh Board exam ka koi bhi twist question rok nahi sakta.

Applies to: Electrostatics (Electric Dipole), Current Electricity, Magnetism (Biot-Savart vs Ampere's law conditions).
