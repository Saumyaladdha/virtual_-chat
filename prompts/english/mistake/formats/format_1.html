⚡ Physics Sign convention Common mistake
Concave mirror mein sign bhoolna ek bahut common galti hai
Dhyan rakhein — concave mirror dekhte hi f ko negative likhein. Yahi step aksar chhoot jaata hai.
Galat
f = +15 cm
Sign nahi lagaya
Sahi
f = −15 cm
Negative sign zaroori hai
Rule to remember
Concave mirror → f hamesha negative.
Convex mirror → f hamesha positive.
Ise ek baar pakka kar lein, aur yeh galti dobara nahi hogi.
